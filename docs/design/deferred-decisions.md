# Deferred directions and revisit conditions

These are recorded options, not implementation commitments. Issues marked `status/deferred` preserve bounded investigations where an explicit use case exists, including their reason and concrete revisit trigger; keep them outside the actionable backlog. Preserve essential context in the issue before deleting source documentation. Do not turn the rest of an old survey into a backlog automatically.

- **Identity floor:** JWK SSH issuance exists. Revisit raw-key restrictions, certificate-lifetime/revocation policy, and a Perses fallback login when external ingress or stronger operator-access restrictions are planned. Keep Authelia/lldap foundational; moving them into k3s was rejected.
- **CA recovery and rotation:** the [CA recovery issue](https://forgejo.internal/mutantmell-net/dotfiles/issues/247) is selected work imported with `status/todo`. Establish expiry dates and the last successful isolated restore now; absent restore evidence prioritizes the audit. Subsequent review is due before a material CA infrastructure change or expansion of certificate-dependent services, when any CA certificate is within 12 months of expiry, or when isolated restore evidence is more than 12 months old—whichever occurs first. Record the next review deadline.
- **Deno scripting:** no repository-wide language migration is approved. Bash/Python remain current. Revisit a single representative offline Nix-packaged TypeScript/Deno pilot when packaging and maintenance benefits can be demonstrated. The contradictory vendoring/source-bundle sketches were proposals, not established conventions.
- **nix-snapshotter:** do not enable on production by default. Revalidate the exact pinned k3s/containerd versions and the old file-valued-store-path failure report in disposable VMs, then runtime/GC/isolation compatibility. Keep ordinary OCI publication for portable images and DevPod until proven otherwise. The old upstream issue number/version snapshot is not a current compatibility claim.
- **Workstations:** edith and trista remain long-lived mutable workstations. The proposed KubeVirt move keeps edith on calvard in a separate cluster and trista on erebonia; both require real VM semantics, durable storage, and recovery. The Kata full-NixOS-container experiment was abandoned because systemd/cgroup requirements conflicted with its agent model.
- **Friend access:** internal Headscale with pre-auth-key enrollment is the planned pilot, restricted to explicit game destinations. Public control/DERP ingress, cloud placement, desktop-hosted sessions, and operator OIDC are separate later decisions. Do not restore removed langport or Keycloak assumptions.
- **General service ideas:** file sync, bookmarks, recipes, wiki, dashboards, Agones, multi-node/HA, web IDEs, and registry replacement remain unselected ideas. Resume only for an actual operator need; product rankings and old purchase prices are not durable documentation.
- **Dev platform:** SSH/editor access and wrapper inventory precede an always-on web UI. A custom DevPod provider, multiple workspaces per VM, and smaller default VMs need demonstrated capacity/usage and reliable CI. Operator-configured connectors and durable OAuth credentials were accepted tradeoffs; do not silently impose the previously rejected blanket connector lockout.
- **Dynamic WireGuard configuration:** the old runtime topology engine was dropped. Add a narrowly scoped endpoint refresh only if current peers demonstrate a need; prefer solving friend enrollment through the selected access model.

The selected friend-access pilot requires **independent Nix-owned subnet-router forwarding policy**, not Headscale ACLs alone. A compromised control plane must not be able to widen access to same-zone services by changing ACLs/routes. [Routing acceptance](https://forgejo.internal/mutantmell-net/dotfiles/issues/263) tests the host firewall with deliberately permissive control-plane policy in a disposable topology.

The inner devcontainer’s [immutable image identity](https://forgejo.internal/mutantmell-net/dotfiles/issues/279) is selected correctness work independent of the deferred nix-snapshotter/provider projects. Upload deduplication does not establish content identity.

OpenWISP is selected for OpenWrt image and configuration management. Its deployment is gated on a stable Arteria/Flint 2/BT8Bridge baseline and trusted calvard infrastructure hosting; the product choice is not deferred. See [ownership and rollout contract](../arteria-gateway.md#selected-openwisp-management-deployment-gated) and the [issue index](../work-tracking.md).
